An update to [brAInStudio 0.1.7](https://github.com/itubrainlab/brainstudio/releases/tag/v0.1.7).

## New

- **Preprocessing per subject.** When several subjects are loaded together, each gets
  its own bad channels, ICA, removed components and interpolation; the Preprocessing
  tab has a subject selector. Subjects are processed one after another, straight into
  one recording. With several subjects, bad channels are interpolated by default
  (a channel bad in any subject would otherwise be left out of every analysis).
  Exported scripts preprocess each subject the same way.
- **One subject at a time in the viewer.** The EEG traces, the gaze plot and the
  overview show only the current subject, with only the channels good in that
  subject. The viewer's and Preprocessing's subject choices are one; double-clicking
  an event of another subject switches to it. The overview shades the subject's runs
  and sessions.
- **Event filter** in the Dataset panel: conditions of column (or any column), test
  (contains, is, starts with, >, ≥, <, ≤, is empty, and their negations) and value,
  met all or any. **Apply** makes the markers it keeps the ones used everywhere: the
  EEG and gaze views, stimuli, epoching and exported scripts (gaze events are kept).
- **Epoch groups are named** from their events and filters; a name you type is kept.
- **Progress bar** with the run or subject being read, searched for gaze events,
  measured for bad channels, fitted with ICA or preprocessed.
- **Gaze at different rates**: files, runs or participants with different gaze rates
  are upsampled to the highest rate (linear; the eye tracker's fixation channels from
  the nearest sample, so its fixations keep their start). Each joined part is
  detected on its own.
- **The eye tracker's own fixations** (Gazepoint `FPOG*`) are used when available, in
  the app and in exported scripts (Controls → Gaze event detection).
- **BIDS run selection**: deselecting a run or session deselects its subject, so it is
  no longer loaded; runs missing a signal the other runs have (EEG or gaze) are greyed
  out, listed in a warning and left out of combined loads.

## Faster, with less memory

- Joining runs keeps about one copy of the EEG: a 268-run dataset (62 h of EEG, 29 GB
  in memory) loads with a 46 GB peak, where 0.1.7 needed 80–100 GB.
- High-rate EEG runs are resampled to the common rate as they are read, with polyphase
  filtering (also more accurate than the FFT method in the 0–40 Hz band).
- Preprocessing no longer copies the whole recording to filter, re-reference, label
  ICA components or compute spectra (identical results). AutoReject, when it picks
  the 1 s segments ICA is fitted on, is fitted on at most 10,000 of them.

## Fixes

- **BIDS eye tracking**: Gazepoint `*_recording-gaze_physio.tsv.gz` files were not
  loaded next to a video stream; `events.tsv` at the subject level was not found; a
  sampling period written as the rate is corrected.
- Exported scripts filter EOG/ECG/EMG channels as the app does.

## Downloads

| System | File | Start |
|---|---|---|
| macOS (Apple Silicon) | `brAInStudio-0.1.8-macos-arm64.dmg` | open the DMG and drag brAInStudio to Applications; the first time, right-click → **Open** (the app is not notarized) |
| Windows (x86_64) | `brAInStudio-0.1.8-windows-x86_64.zip` | unzip and run `brAInStudio\brAInStudio.exe` (SmartScreen may ask you to confirm) |
| Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.35+) | `brAInStudio-0.1.8-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | unpack and run `brAInStudio/brAInStudio` |
| Python | `brainstudio-0.1.8-py3-none-any.whl`, sdist | `pip install brainstudio-0.1.8-py3-none-any.whl` (Python ≥ 3.12) |

Each app was checked on its own system with `brAInStudio --self-test`. The self-test
loads a synthetic EEG and gaze dataset with stimuli, then runs gaze event detection,
stimulus video decoding, preprocessing, ICA with ICLabel, epoching with AutoReject,
script export, ERP component statistics and band power.

Full documentation: [README](https://github.com/itubrainlab/brainstudio#readme).
